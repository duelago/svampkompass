The compass now points to true north. Bearings to a saved spot were compared
against the phone's magnetic compass, which was off by several degrees while
standing still.

The home position can no longer be overwritten by accident; it now asks
first, showing how far away the saved point is.

Deleting a mushroom spot can be undone.

The app no longer gets stuck on a loading spinner when location services
misbehave, and offers a way forward when permission is permanently denied.

Saved places are read more tolerantly: one corrupt entry no longer discards
the rest.
